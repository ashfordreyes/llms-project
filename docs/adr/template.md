# ADR-000: A short sentence naming the decision

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-00X
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision? What constraints are real —
budget, time, what the data looks like, what you already tried?

Write this so it makes sense to someone who has not been in your head. If the
decision was forced by something you discovered, say what you discovered.

## Decision

What you are doing. Present tense, specific. "We use X" — not "we will
probably use X".

## Alternatives considered

**The other real option.** Describe it fairly, including what would have been
good about it. Then say why you did not choose it.

**A second one, if there was one.** Same.

If you genuinely had no alternative, that is a signal this is not a decision
and does not need an ADR.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, and what it makes harder. The second half is the one
that gets skipped and the one that is worth reading.

Include what would make you revisit this. A decision you cannot imagine
reversing was probably not a decision.
